About Whitefish

Built around verifiable opportunities.

Whitefish Exploration is an independent oil and gas project development and advisory company. We work with asset owners, operators, technical professionals, service companies, and project partners to evaluate opportunities and help move viable projects toward execution.

Our approach combines technical research, public-record verification, field-level due diligence, financial analysis, project coordination, and clearly documented ownership and operating structures.

Capabilities

From first review to a practical development plan.

01

Project Identification

Producing, shut-in, underdeveloped, and redevelopment opportunities with existing wells, leases, equipment, gathering systems, or infrastructure.

02

Technical Evaluation

Well histories, production records, geological data, mud logs, completion records, formation targets, offsets, infrastructure, and regulatory information.

03

Project Development

Practical workover, recompletion, re-entry, completion, and phased field-development strategies coordinated with qualified operators and technical professionals.

04

Transaction Support

Due diligence, ownership verification, project documentation, economic modeling, stakeholder coordination, and organized information for informed decisions.
